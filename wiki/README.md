## The whole thing on one page

1. **Name a subject** and pick a depth: Cursory Dive (~5 h), Boot Camp (~12 h) or Quarter (~27 h) ([Making a course](making-a-course.md)).
2. **Answer a few questions** about what draws you to it, or skip them.
3. **Review the plan**: approve it, or ask for changes in plain words.
4. **Study** whenever you like: session 1 is ready in a minute or two while the rest are written ([Studying](studying.md)).
   Just want to read? Switch on **Reading mode** and the questions step aside.
5. **Ask the tutor** whenever something doesn't click ([The tutor](the-tutor.md)).
6. **Fix what you'd like different**: right-click a section to edit or rewrite it ([Editing a course](editing-courses.md)).

No deadlines, no notifications, no grades or scores: just progress, and what's worth another look.
